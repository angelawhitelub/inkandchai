Refund Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026  ·  inkandchai.in

Refunds

We will notify you once we’ve received and inspected your return, and let you know if the refund was approved or not.

Once approved, how the money reaches you depends on how you paid. An online payment can be reversed; cash cannot, so for any order where you paid cash we need you to tell us where to send it.

How your refund reaches you

  1. Paid online in full (card, UPI, net banking). The refund goes back to the same account or card automatically, once the returned book reaches us. Nothing is needed from you. It is usually credited in 2–4 business days, though some banks take up to 10.
  2. Cash on Delivery. Nothing was paid online, so there is no payment for us to reverse. We transfer the refund to a UPI ID, or to a bank account (account number, IFSC and account-holder name), that you give us when you raise the return. We send it after the returned book reaches us.
  3. Partial COD (deposit online, balance in cash). Only the deposit was taken online — typically about a tenth of the order — so the payment gateway cannot return the rest. We send you the full amount you paid, deposit and cash together, as a single transfer to the UPI ID or bank account you give us. We do not split it into two refunds.
  4. Store credit, if you prefer it. On any order you can choose Ink & Chai store credit instead of money back. It is issued immediately, carries a ₹50 bonus on top of your refund, is valid for 6 months, and needs no bank details. You do not have to wait for the book to reach us.

If you paid by COD or Partial COD, we cannot complete your return without payout details.

When you raise the return you will be asked for either a UPI ID (like name@bank) or your bank account number together with the IFSC code and the account-holder’s name. Either one is enough — UPI is faster.

We ask for this up front on purpose. Without it there is no way to send your money, and the refund would sit waiting indefinitely.

When the money is sent

  1. Online payments: the refund is initiated within 24 hours of the returned book passing inspection, and credited by your bank in 2–4 business days.
  2. COD and Partial COD: we transfer within 3–5 business days of the returned book reaching us and passing inspection. We do not pay out before the book is back with us.
  3. Store credit: issued immediately when you raise the return.

eBooks are non-refundable

eBooks cannot be returned, cancelled or refunded once purchased. An eBook is delivered the moment your payment succeeds — the book opens in your browser straight away — so there is nothing to send back and no dispatch to stop. This is told to you before you pay, on the eBook itself and again in the payment window.

Before buying, use Read sample on any eBook. It shows you the first five pages free, so you can check the book and that it reads properly on your device.

If an eBook genuinely will not open for you, or you were charged twice for the same title, write to refunds@inkandchai.in and we will sort it out. A duplicate charge for a book you already own is always returned.

This applies only to eBooks. Printed books follow the rest of this policy as usual.

Parcels returned to us

If the customer refuses to accept the order and the parcel is returned to us, we will deduct the shipping charge at the time of refund.

If the customer puts a wrong contact number or wrong PIN code and the parcel is returned to us, we will deduct the shipping charge at the time of refund.

Claiming a return, refund or replacement

Customers are requested to drop a mail at refunds@inkandchai.in to claim returns, refunds or replacements.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel from My Orders. What is possible depends on how you paid and how far the order has got:

  1. Cash on Delivery: cancel any time before the parcel is dispatched. Nothing was paid, so there is nothing to refund.
  2. Paid online in full: cancel within 30 minutes of placing the order, and we refund the full amount to the original payment method.
  3. Partial COD: cancel within 30 minutes of placing the order and before dispatch. The deposit you paid online is refunded to the same account or card.

Once an order has been released for dispatch it can no longer be cancelled. You can refuse the parcel at the door, or raise a return once it is delivered — see the Return Policy.
