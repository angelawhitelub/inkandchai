Report a Copyright Issue

We respect the rights of authors, publishers and artists  ·  inkandchai.in

Ink & Chai respects the intellectual property of others and expects the same of everyone who sells or shares through our store. If you believe a product page on inkandchai.in uses your copyrighted work without permission — a book, its cover, artwork, photographs, a description or any other content — please tell us using the form below.

What we do with a report

  • Every report is reviewed and answered by email within 72 hours of being sent.
  • Where a report is valid we act on it. That can include removing the product page, stopping sale of the item, removing images or text, and taking further action against repeat infringement.
  • We may write to you for more information, and we may share the report with the party who provided the content so they can respond.
  • You will get a reference number by email as soon as you send the report.
