Our location

Ink & Chai is an online bookstore operated from 2379 Kucha Mir Hashim, Turkman Gate, Delhi – 110006, India. We ship pan-India from our Delhi warehouse. As an online-only retailer, we don't have a physical walk-in store, but our team is always reachable by email or WhatsApp.

Track your order

Already placed an order? You can track it any time from the Track Order page or the My Orders section of your account on inkandchai.in. Shipping and delivery updates are also sent to you by email and WhatsApp.

Defective, wrong or missing book

Return any book within 7 days of delivery for a full refund: sign in, open My Orders, choose Return or replace → Return for a refund, and we collect it from you. A book that arrived defective, misprinted, wrong or missing is replaced free — from My Orders or the Track Order page. Full details are on our Returns & Replacements and Refund Policy pages.
