Questions

Can I read a bit before buying?
Yes — every eBook has a free sample of the opening pages, and you do not need an account to read it.

Can I read it on my phone?
Yes. It opens in your browser on any phone, tablet or computer. There is nothing to install.

Can I download the file?
No. eBooks are read here on the site rather than handed over as a file, which is how we can keep selling them at these prices. Your copy is always in your account — sign in from any device and it is there.

Are eBooks returnable?
Because there is nothing to send back, eBook sales are final — please use the free sample first. If a book will not open or is the wrong one, message us on WhatsApp and we will put it right.
